Punishment Classification System

The following classification system is used to determine appropriate punishments for rule violations. Staff members must adhere to these guidelines when issuing penalties.

Class Description Punishment Examples
Class A Minor infractions that disrupt roleplay or violate basic rules Warning Minor metagaming, accidental powergaming, minor chat violations
Class B Moderate infractions or repeated Class A violations Temporary Ban (1-5 days, 1 day per offense) RDM, VDM, severe metagaming, repeated powergaming
Class C Serious infractions or repeated Class B violations Appealable Permanent Ban Severe harassment, extreme RDM/VDM, repeated rule violations after temporary bans
Class D Critical infractions that severely impact the community Non-Appealable Permanent Ban Exploiting, hacking, doxxing, real-life threats, hate speech

General Rules

G1: Respect All Players – Harassment, discrimination, or bullying is not tolerated.

All players must be treated with respect regardless of their background, identity, or roleplay style. This includes refraining from personal attacks, discriminatory language, or targeted harassment.

Examples of violations:

  • Using slurs or derogatory language
  • Targeting specific players repeatedly
  • Making discriminatory comments about race, gender, sexuality, etc.
G2: Follow Roblox ToS – Comply with Roblox Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

All staff members must adhere to the official Roblox Terms of Service and Community Guidelines at all times. Violations may result in both in-game penalties and Roblox platform penalties.

Key points:

  • No discussion of illegal activities
  • No sharing personal information
  • No bypassing chat filters
  • No discussion of dating or romantic relationships
G3: Staff Instructions – Follow staff directions. Disputes must be handled privately afterward.

Players must follow instructions from staff members. If a player disagrees with a staff decision, they must comply in the moment and address concerns through proper channels afterward.

Proper dispute resolution:

  • Submit a formal appeal through the designated Discord channel
  • Provide evidence and context for the dispute
  • Wait for a response from senior staff
G4: Appropriate Content – No explicit, offensive, or NSFW content in usernames, chat, or avatars.

All content visible to other players must be appropriate for all ages. This includes usernames, chat messages, avatars, and any other user-generated content.

Prohibited content includes:

  • Sexual or suggestive content
  • Graphic violence or gore
  • Drug references
  • Offensive symbols or imagery
G5: No Exploiting – Use of hacks, glitches, or unauthorized tools results in immediate ban.

Players must not use any unauthorized methods to gain advantages or disrupt gameplay. This includes hacks, exploits, glitches, or third-party tools.

Examples of exploiting:

  • Using speed hacks or noclip
  • Exploiting game mechanics to bypass intended limitations
  • Using auto-clickers or macros
  • Intentionally causing server lag or crashes

Roleplay Rules

RP1: Stay In Character – Remain in-character during gameplay. Use /b for out-of-character chat.

Players must maintain their character's persona during roleplay. Out-of-character (OOC) communication should be kept to a minimum and only used when necessary through the appropriate channels.

Example:

In-Character: "Officer, I was just trying to get to the hospital quickly because my friend is hurt."

Out-of-Character (using /b): "/b Hey, my game is lagging, give me a second."

RP2: No Metagaming – Do not use OOC knowledge in character decisions.

Metagaming occurs when a player uses information their character wouldn't realistically know to gain an advantage in roleplay. All character decisions must be based solely on information obtained through in-character interactions.

Examples:

Not Allowed: Using Discord chat information in-game, recognizing a player by their username rather than their character, or acting on information from outside the current roleplay scenario.

Allowed: Only acting on information your character has directly witnessed or been told about in-game.

RP3: No Powergaming – Avoid unrealistic actions and allow fair roleplay interaction.

Powergaming occurs when a player forces actions or outcomes on others without giving them a fair chance to respond or react. All interactions should allow for realistic responses and fair roleplay.

Examples:

Not Allowed: "I shoot you in the head and you die instantly" or "I dodge all your bullets and knock you out with one punch."

Allowed: "I aim my weapon at you" (allowing the other player to respond) or "I attempt to punch you" (giving the other player a chance to react).

RP4: Value Your Life – Avoid actions that disregard character life value.

Players must roleplay as if their character values their life. This means avoiding unnecessarily dangerous situations and responding realistically to threats.

Examples:

Not Allowed: Continuing to fight when severely outnumbered or outgunned, or taunting someone who has a weapon pointed at you.

Allowed: Surrendering when at gunpoint, seeking medical attention when injured, or avoiding unnecessarily dangerous situations.

RP5: Realistic Roleplay – Keep actions grounded in realistic expectations.

All roleplay actions should be realistic and plausible within the context of the game world. Avoid superhuman abilities or unrealistic scenarios.

Examples of Self-Harm Roleplay:

Not Allowed (Self-Harm RP): "I'm going to jump! Back off!" (threatening self-harm as a roleplay tactic)

Allowed: "I'm going to jump off of this bridge so my character can escape!" (jumping as a means of escape, not self-harm)

RP6: No RDM – Killing without valid RP reason is prohibited.

Random Death Match (RDM) occurs when a player kills another player without any valid roleplay reason or prior interaction. All hostile actions must have proper roleplay justification.

Examples:

Not Allowed: Killing random players without interaction, or killing someone over a minor verbal disagreement.

Allowed: Killing in self-defense, as part of a properly initiated gang conflict, or during a robbery gone wrong after proper roleplay interaction.

RP7: No VDM – Vehicles must not be used as weapons without roleplay.

Vehicle Death Match (VDM) occurs when a player uses a vehicle as a weapon to kill or injure other players without proper roleplay justification.

Examples:

Not Allowed: Deliberately running over players on the sidewalk, ramming other vehicles without roleplay reason.

Allowed: Using a vehicle as part of a properly initiated chase scenario, or accidentally hitting someone during a high-stress roleplay situation.

RP8: Proper Scenarios – Avoid exaggerated or illogical roleplay scenarios.

Roleplay scenarios should be logical, realistic, and appropriate for the game world. Avoid creating situations that are overly dramatic, impossible, or disruptive to the overall roleplay environment.

Examples:

Not Allowed: Claiming to be an alien, having superpowers, or creating scenarios that would be impossible in a realistic setting.

Allowed: Creating scenarios that fit within the game's setting and lore, such as realistic criminal activities, business ventures, or social interactions.

Communication Rules

C1: Roleplay Chat – Use local chat for character dialogue only.

Local chat should only be used for in-character dialogue. All communication in this channel should be from the perspective of your character.

Examples:

Allowed: "Hey, do you know where the nearest gas station is?" (in-character question)

Not Allowed: "Can someone help me with the controls?" (out-of-character question)

C2: OOC Communication – Minimize OOC chat and keep it respectful.

Out-of-character (OOC) communication should be kept to a minimum and only used when necessary. When using OOC chat, keep it respectful and relevant.

Examples:

Allowed OOC: "/b My game is lagging, give me a moment please."

Not Allowed OOC: "/b This server is boring" or excessive OOC chatting that disrupts roleplay.

C3: No Mic Spam – No disruptive or irrelevant voice activity.

Voice chat must be used appropriately without spamming, playing music, or making disruptive noises. Voice communication should enhance roleplay, not disrupt it.

Examples of prohibited behavior:

  • Playing music through microphone
  • Making loud or disruptive noises
  • Talking over others repeatedly
  • Using voice changers disruptively
C4: Language – Maintain appropriate language. No offensive or excessive profanity.

While some mild profanity may be acceptable in appropriate roleplay contexts, excessive or offensive language is prohibited. All communication should remain appropriate for a diverse community.

Guidelines:

  • Mild profanity is allowed in appropriate contexts
  • No slurs or deeply offensive language
  • No excessive profanity that disrupts roleplay
  • Context-appropriate language (e.g., professional in professional settings)
C5: Emergency Services – Emergency lines must only be used for RP emergencies.

Emergency service communication channels (911, police radio, etc.) should only be used for legitimate roleplay emergencies. Misuse of these channels disrupts emergency service roleplay.

Examples:

Allowed: Calling 911 to report a robbery, accident, or medical emergency.

Not Allowed: Calling 911 as a prank, to ask non-emergency questions, or to harass emergency services.

Vehicle Rules

V1: Vehicle Operation – Drive responsibly within RP. Break rules only with RP context.

Vehicles should be operated in a manner consistent with realistic driving behavior. Traffic laws should generally be followed unless there is a specific roleplay reason to break them.

Examples:

Allowed: Speeding during a chase, running red lights while rushing to the hospital in an emergency.

Not Allowed: Driving recklessly without roleplay reason, intentionally causing accidents for fun.

V2: Vehicle Theft – Must have RP justification and process.

Vehicle theft must have proper roleplay justification and follow a realistic process. Random theft without roleplay is prohibited.

Proper vehicle theft process:

  1. Establish roleplay reason for the theft
  2. Attempt to gain access to the vehicle through roleplay (lockpicking, carjacking, etc.)
  3. Allow for potential intervention or response from the vehicle owner or law enforcement
V3: Property Respect – RP is required for any property damage or trespass.

Damaging property or trespassing must have proper roleplay justification. Random property damage or trespassing without roleplay context is prohibited.

Examples:

Allowed: Breaking into a building as part of a planned robbery, damaging a vehicle during a chase.

Not Allowed: Randomly breaking windows, entering private property without roleplay reason.

V4: Parking – Vehicles must not obstruct essential access areas.

Vehicles should be parked in appropriate locations and should not obstruct essential areas such as entrances, emergency vehicle access points, or high-traffic areas.

Prohibited parking:

  • Blocking hospital entrances
  • Blocking police station access
  • Parking in the middle of roads
  • Intentionally blocking other players' vehicles

Faction Rules

F1: Job Requirements – Follow duties and regulations tied to your faction or job.

Players must adhere to the specific requirements and regulations of their chosen faction or job. Each role has specific responsibilities that must be fulfilled.

Examples:

  • Police officers must enforce laws and protect citizens
  • Medical personnel must provide healthcare to all patients
  • Government officials must follow proper procedures
  • Criminal organizations must maintain their hierarchy and operations
F2: Corruption – Staff approval required for corruption RP.

Roleplay involving corruption (such as corrupt police officers or government officials) requires prior approval from staff. This ensures that corruption roleplay is balanced and does not negatively impact the server.

Corruption approval process:

  1. Submit a detailed plan to staff through the designated channel
  2. Include the scope, duration, and purpose of the corruption roleplay
  3. Wait for explicit approval before beginning
  4. Follow any limitations or guidelines provided by staff
F3: Criminal Activity – Must be well-planned and follow realistic execution.

Criminal activities must be well-planned and executed in a realistic manner. Random or poorly planned criminal activities that disrupt roleplay are prohibited.

Guidelines for criminal roleplay:

  • Plan activities thoroughly before execution
  • Consider potential consequences and responses
  • Maintain realistic expectations about success and failure
  • Allow for appropriate law enforcement response
F4: Gang/Group Conflicts – RP must justify conflicts. No random attacks.

Conflicts between gangs or groups must have proper roleplay justification. Random attacks or "war declarations" without prior roleplay context are prohibited.

Proper conflict development:

  1. Establish roleplay tension or disagreement between groups
  2. Attempt negotiation or resolution through roleplay
  3. Escalate gradually if resolution fails
  4. Maintain realistic scope and intensity of conflict

Administration Rules

A1: Reporting Issues – Submit reports with evidence to staff or Community Safety Associates.

All issues requiring staff intervention must be properly reported through the designated channels with sufficient evidence. This ensures that staff can address problems efficiently and fairly.

Proper reporting process:

  1. Gather evidence (screenshots, recordings, timestamps)
  2. Submit a report through the designated Discord channel
  3. Include all relevant details (players involved, time, server)
  4. Wait for staff response without discussing the report publicly
A2: Staff Decisions – All staff decisions are final unless formally appealed.

Decisions made by staff members are considered final and must be respected. If a player believes a decision was unfair, they may submit a formal appeal through the proper channels.

Appeal process:

  1. Submit a formal appeal through the designated Discord channel
  2. Include all relevant information and evidence
  3. Maintain respectful communication throughout the process
  4. Accept the final decision after the appeal has been reviewed

Penalty System

P1: Warning System – Minor infractions lead to warnings; repeated offenses escalate.

Warnings are issued for minor infractions. Accumulating multiple warnings will result in more severe penalties. Warnings are tracked and may affect future penalty decisions.

Warning progression:

  • First offense: Verbal warning
  • Second offense: Formal warning (documented)
  • Third offense: Final warning with potential temporary restrictions
  • Further offenses: Escalation to temporary or permanent ban
P2: Temporary Bans – Enforced for repeat or serious rule violations.

Temporary bans are issued for repeated minor infractions or moderate rule violations. The duration depends on the severity and frequency of the violations.

Temporary ban durations:

  • 1 day: First temporary ban or minor repeated offense
  • 3 days: Second temporary ban or moderate offense
  • 5 days: Third temporary ban or serious offense
  • 7+ days: Multiple temporary bans or very serious offense
P3: Permanent Bans – Applied to severe or persistent rule-breaking behavior.

Permanent bans are issued for severe rule violations or persistent rule-breaking despite previous penalties. Some permanent bans may be appealable, while others are not.

Permanent ban categories:

  • Appealable: Severe but potentially forgivable violations
  • Non-appealable: Critical violations that fundamentally harm the community

Discord Moderation Commands

The following commands are available for staff to use in the Discord server for moderation purposes:

Command Description Usage
/ban [username] [duration] [reason] Bans a user from the Roblox game /ban JohnDoe 7d Repeated RDM violations
/warn [username] [reason] Issues a formal warning to a user /warn JaneDoe Inappropriate language in chat
/note [username] [context] Adds a note to a user's record without issuing a penalty /note PlayerXYZ Player was advised about proper RP etiquette

Important Guidelines for Command Usage:

  • Always provide clear and specific reasons for warnings and bans
  • Document all moderation actions in the appropriate channels
  • Use the appropriate penalty class (A, B, C, D) when issuing penalties
  • Consult with senior staff for guidance on complex situations

In-Game Staff Commands

The following commands are available for staff to use in-game:

Command Description Usage Context
:fly Enables flight mode for staff Use for monitoring situations from above or reaching inaccessible areas
:ff Enables forcefield (makes staff invulnerable) Use when moderating potentially dangerous situations
:unff Disables forcefield Use to return to normal vulnerability state
:to [username] Teleports staff to specified player Use to quickly respond to reports or check on suspicious activity
:bring [username] Teleports specified player to staff Use for private discussions or removing players from problematic situations
:m [message] Announces a message server-wide Use for important announcements or server-wide warnings
:view [username] Allows staff to see from player's perspective Use for investigating potential rule violations or monitoring suspicious activity
:god Enables god mode (invulnerability and special abilities) Use only in extreme situations requiring immediate intervention
:team [username] [team name] Changes a player's team assignment Use to correct team assignments or for administrative purposes

Command Usage Guidelines:

  • Only use staff commands when necessary for moderation purposes
  • Avoid using commands in front of players when possible to maintain immersion
  • Document any significant command usage in staff logs
  • Never use commands for personal advantage or non-administrative purposes
